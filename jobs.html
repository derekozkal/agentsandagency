Jobs that didn't exist in 2022

A running list of work that wasn't possible before frontier models, as opposed to old jobs with new tools. Each entry says what the job is and why it couldn't exist earlier. Some have postings today, and some are still hypotheses.

Column 01

AI does the task

Automation. The worker moves on, up, or out. This is the most studied case.

Column 02

AI helps with the task

Augmentation. The worker gets more done. Most current research and hype sits here.

Column 03

AI makes new tasks possible

New work that wasn't affordable or possible before. It's barely measured, and this list sits here.

The list

18 entries · last reviewed July 2026

CONTEXT

Expertise Encoder

Sits with a retiring machinist, nurse, or loan officer and turns thirty years of judgment into a corpus a model can actually use.

No system could hold, let alone apply, a career of unstructured judgment.

GENERALITY

Evals Designer

Writes the exams for AI systems. Decides what "good" means for software with no spec sheet, then measures it.

Software did what it was told. There was nothing open-ended to examine.

AGENCY

Agent Operations Lead

Runs a fleet of autonomous agents the way a plant manager runs a line: throughput, error budgets, escalation paths.

Software never acted on its own long enough to need an operations discipline.

CONTEXT

Context Engineer

Designs what a model sees before it answers: the documents, the ordering, the framing. Real job postings exist today.

Nothing could read a million tokens, so nobody curated a million tokens.

GENERALITY

AI Output Auditor

Checks machine output for a living: provenance, calibration, failure modes. Quality control for cognition.

Output volume was human-scale and mostly self-verifying.

COST

Synthetic Data Curator

Builds training data that never happened to teach models things that did. Data design as a full-time craft.

Generating data used to cost more than collecting it.

COST

Counterfactual Analyst

Runs a policy through a thousand simulated alternate versions before a legislature votes once.

Each scenario used to take a team a quarter. Now the marginal one is nearly free.

CONTEXT

Institutional Translator

Makes an organization’s unwritten rules legible enough for agents to follow them.

The unwritten rules could not run on anything.

GENERALITY

Model Welfare Researcher

Studies whether and when AI systems warrant moral consideration. A real research program at real labs.

Nothing artificial was a serious enough candidate to study.

AGENCY

AI Red Teamer

Attacks models professionally so the failures get found on payroll instead of in production.

Adversarial testing meant networks and locks, not minds.

AGENCY

Agent Economist

Designs market rules for autonomous buyers, sellers, and negotiators. Mechanism design goes operational.

Markets had no non-human participants with budgets and goals.

CODE

Curriculum Compiler

Turns a master practitioner’s method into a teachable, testable agent.

Teaching could not be compiled.

COST

Simulation Demographer

Builds synthetic populations so a city can test a policy on everyone before trying it on anyone.

Population-scale simulation was a supercomputer grant, not a Tuesday.

AGENCY

Deep Research Editor

Assigns, supervises, and edits a research staff that never sleeps. The judgment moves up a level, and the reading moves down.

An editor’s staff was limited to people.

CONTEXT

Memory Architect

Decides what a long-lived agent keeps, forgets, and consolidates. Institutional memory as a built system.

Software had storage. It did not have memory worth architecting.

MULTIMODAL

Multimodal Field Auditor

Points a model at bridge photos, shop-floor video, or crop imagery and gets structured findings back.

Seeing was not something you could delegate.

GENERALITY

Capability Forecaster

Tracks the frontier and tells institutions what becomes possible next quarter, not next decade.

The frontier moved slowly enough that strategy could ignore it.

GENERALITY

Preference Elicitor

Turns messy human values into training signal a model can learn from.

There was no system that could take values as an input.
