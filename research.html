Initiatives

Work I helped start

  • Research lineage

    NSF National Network of Research Institutes workshop

    I was the invited philanthropy participant in the 2021 NSF workshop on regional and place-based innovation. NSF cites the workshop as part of the groundwork for its Regional Innovation Engines program, the largest place-based R&D investment since the land-grant acts. In July 2026 the program funded the UMKC-led Critical Materials Crossroads Engine in Kansas City, up to $160M over ten years. Workshop report.

  • Signature program

    The Kauffman Knowledge Challenges (2018, 2020, 2022)

    Kauffman's main research calls on entrepreneurship and the economy. I helped design and run them: 1,100+ submissions and $12M across 60+ projects. The 2018 grantee announcement includes a quote from me.

  • Field-building

    The Future of Work research program (2016 on)

    I wrote the 2016 call for proposals that launched Kauffman's future-of-work research funding, years before the topic drew wide attention. Its convenings and commissioned work ran for several years, and related papers are still coming out.

  • Policy platform

    America's New Business Plan

    I contributed to Kauffman's bipartisan entrepreneurship policy platform, built with the Start Us Up coalition of 130+ organizations. It lays out ways to reduce barriers to starting a business. The plan.
