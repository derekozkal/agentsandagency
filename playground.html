The Playground

Four browser games I built with AI. They're free, need no account, and work on a phone.

New

Chains & Chaos

Disc golf at Heritage Park and Water Works, built from the real UDisc layouts. Draft from 20 players, including six KC locals. Pick a disc and a line, thread the gold gaps, and play your wild cards. One skips your disc across a pond. Another hands it to a duck.

Play Chains & Chaos

Herd Mentality

Laser-horned unicorns defend a rainbow bridge from the Grumps, an army of gray clouds. Drag to steer and grow the herd. Bosses include The Monday and Per My Last Email.

Play Herd Mentality

Math Grove

A summer math tutor I built for my daughters, open to anyone. Solve problems, earn coins, and grow a garden. Tracks for kids entering 3rd and 6th grade. No ads.

Open Math Grove

Directorate

Run a covert agency from 1972 to 2026. Work field leads and stop conspiracies before they execute. A nod to Covert Action, SimCity, and Age of Empires II. Plays in one sitting.

Open Directorate
