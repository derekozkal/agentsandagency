The Arcade

Four browser games built with AI. All are free, need no account, and work on a phone.

New

Chains & Chaos

Disc golf at Heritage Park and Water Works, with hole lengths and pars from the published UDisc layouts. Draft from 20 players, including six KC locals. Murph gets a stroke back after three straight pars. TC has tattoos and extra power. Pick a disc, its stability, and a high or low line, then thread the gold gaps for a bonus. Each player gets ten face-down wild cards. One might skip your disc across a pond. Another hands it to a duck.

Play Chains & Chaos

Herd Mentality

A herd of laser-horned unicorns defends a rainbow bridge from the Grumps, an army of gray clouds. Drag to steer, shoot gates to raise their numbers, and run through the better one. A Grump you hit floats away happy. Bosses include The Monday and Per My Last Email. Levels don't end, and progress saves in your browser.

Play Herd Mentality

Math Grove

A summer math tutor built for my daughters and open to anyone. Solve problems, earn coins, and grow a garden that's all yours. Two grade bridges, entering 3rd grade and entering 6th grade, each with 8 topics and 3 tiers. Clear all three tiers to earn a star. There's also a bonus Snake game played inside the garden you grew. No account, no ads, saves in your browser.

Open Math Grove

Directorate

Run a covert agency across four eras, 1972 to 2026. Build headquarters, work field leads, solve conspiracies before they execute, and manage global tension. Eight minigames feed a strategy layer. Influenced by some of my favorite games as a kid, like Covert Action, SimCity, Conflict, and Age of Empires II. One sitting, no save state.

Open Directorate
Verified by consumption · replication ongoing

The Kitchen

I spend my working hours on human-AI collaboration, so it seems only fair to disclose my own. These were made by me, with AI as sous-chef for recipes, ratios, and troubleshooting. Peer review was conducted by my family, and the braided cinnamon apple bread got a "revise and resubmit." So I made an apple pie, which went much better.

Findings so far: AI is a good cooking partner. It's patient with basic questions, happy to help at 10 p.m., and wrong about an oven temperature about once per project. Also in the rotation: veggie burgers, stews, and a cheesecake. Methodology shared on request.
