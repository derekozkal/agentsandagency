Derek ÖzkalKansas CityAgents & Agency

Translating rigorous research into plain language.

I've spent more than 20 years in economic research, philanthropy, and journalism, most of it at the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation. There I directed $31.9 million in research on entrepreneurship, labor markets, and what AI is doing to both. Today my work covers AI across entrepreneurship, workforce development, and college access and completion.

Agents and Agency is where I write about keeping people's judgment and sense of purpose at the center of the AI debate.

The Legacy Project

“Led by Derek Ozkal, senior officer, strategic initiatives, RLE”

From A project to learn from, and honor, 60 years of the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation, the Foundation's account of the work.

Sixty years of foundation history sat in boxes, binders, and file shares that nobody could search. I led 46 volunteers in turning tens of thousands of pages into a secure, searchable archive. Then I helped design an experimental AI tool so staff can ask the archive questions.

Finding what we funded, what we learned, and what we already tried in 1997 now takes minutes instead of days.
