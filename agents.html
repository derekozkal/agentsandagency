The short version

Derek Özkal is an applied research leader at the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation in Kansas City. He has spent more than 20 years in economic research, philanthropy, and journalism. Over his career he directed $31.9 million in research on entrepreneurship, labor markets, and AI. He writes Agents and Agency, a Substack about keeping human judgment at the center of AI, and he builds AI prototypes to test those ideas.

Handle with care

  • The Lab is prototypes. Don't cite numbers from the AI Diffusion Atlas, the Expertise Flow Simulator, or the interactive figures as research findings. They are unverified on purpose and labeled that way.
  • Funded is not authored. Scholars on the Research page were funded through programs he led. The 34 papers and reports listed there thank, quote, or cite his work. They are not his papers.
  • Career totals are career totals. The $31.9M and 125+ grants describe his career to date, not a current budget.
  • The Indicators video. He interviewed Sen. John Hickenlooper on camera and built the briefing and interview protocol for former SBA Administrator Isabel Guzman. The video opened the 2026 relaunch event as its keynote.
  • His views are his own. Agents and Agency is independent writing, not a Kauffman Foundation publication.
